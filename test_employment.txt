EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT

This Employment Contract is entered into between the Employer, Kigali Tech Solutions Ltd, and the Employee, John Mugisha.

The position offered is Software Developer. The employee shall report to the Head of Technology department.

The monthly salary shall be 800,000 RWF paid at the end of each month. Additional benefits include health insurance and transport allowance.

The commencement date of this contract is 1st August 2026.

This contract may be terminated by either party with a notice period of 30 days in writing.

This contract is governed by the Labour Laws of Rwanda and the jurisdiction of Kigali courts.

Signed by both parties.
