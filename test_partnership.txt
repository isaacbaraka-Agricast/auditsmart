PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Partnership Agreement is entered into between the partners, Mr. Eric Mugabo and Mrs. Alice Uwase, for the purpose of operating a technology business together.

The business objective is to provide software development and IT consulting services in Rwanda.

Profits and losses shall be shared equally between the two partners at 50% each.

Capital contribution shall be equal from both partners.

The duration of this partnership is five years starting from 1st August 2026.

This partnership may be dissolved by written notice of 90 days from either partner.

This agreement is governed by the company laws of Rwanda.
