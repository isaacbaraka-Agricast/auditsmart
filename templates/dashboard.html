📖 What do these metrics mean?
Precision — Out of all documents the AI classified as a certain type, how many were actually correct.
Recall — Out of all actual documents of a certain type, how many did the AI correctly identify.
F1-Score — The balance between Precision and Recall. A high F1-Score means the model performs well overall.
Cross-Validation — The model was tested on data it had never seen before to ensure fair and accurate results.
