SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement is entered into between the Client, Rwanda Development Board, and the Service Provider, Digital Solutions Ltd.

Scope of Services: The service provider shall deliver software development and IT consulting services including system design, development and testing.

The client shall pay a fee of 2,500,000 RWF per month for the duration of this agreement.

The term of this agreement is 12 months starting from 1st August 2026.

Either party may terminate this agreement with a written notice of 30 days.

This agreement is governed by the laws of Rwanda and jurisdiction of Kigali.

Signed by both parties.
